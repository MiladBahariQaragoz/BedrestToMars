- At the end of bed rest, the PM muscle volume increased compared with the baseline value ( p = 0.010, 211.3 mm 3 with a 95% CI of [45.8, 376.7]) and was still greater ( p = 0.001, 293.0 mm 3 with a 95% CI of [132.2, 453.2]) in the recovery phase ( Table 2 and Figure 2C ).
- The average reduction in QL muscle volume was 10.5 ± 8.3% (−237.7.6 ± 208.1 mm 3 ) compared with baseline values (both– p < 0.05).
- In the recovery phase, the QL muscle volume showed a strong tendency to return towards baseline values at the L2/L3 IVD level ( p = 0.050, −140.5 mm 3 with a 95% CI of [−281.0, −0.1]) but remained smaller than baseline values at L3/L4 ( p = 0.042, −135.7 mm 3 with a 95% CI of [−267.6, −3.8]) IVD level ( Table 2 and Figure 2D ).
