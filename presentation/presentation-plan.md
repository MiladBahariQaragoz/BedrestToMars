# Technical half of the talk: build plan

**Scope:** the technical half of the DGLRM talk (about 6 minutes, 5 slides), to go after the partner's half (problem, data gathering, meta-regression).
**Source of truth:** the final report on branch `feat/report`. Every number on a slide must match `results/` there.
**Output:** `/mnt/project-files/slides/technical-half.pptx` (16:9), with speaker notes, so the partner can paste the slides into the shared deck.
**Naming rule:** the language model is never called by its product or company name. On slides it is "the LLM" or "a newly released language model built for probabilistic classification".

---

## Phase 1: Content plan
Decide what each slide says, in what order, in how much time.

**Done when:**
- Each slide has one message, written as its title.
- Timings add up to 6 minutes or less.
- Each slide has a named source in the report for its numbers.
- The meta-regression is left to the partner and not repeated.

**Status:** done. See `technical-half-outline.md`.

## Phase 2: Charts without the model's name
The repo's figures (F4 to F9) print the model's product name. Rebuild the needed charts from the results files instead of reusing those images.

**Done when:**
- Every chart is drawn from `results/` on `feat/report` and its numbers can be traced to a file.
- No chart, label, legend or file name contains the model's product or company name.
- The charts are native PowerPoint charts where possible, so the team can restyle them.
- The repository is unchanged (nothing edited, nothing pushed).

**Status:** done. Charts rebuilt as native PowerPoint charts from `results/` (model_comparison.csv, tabpfn_comparison.csv, forecast_comparison.csv, forecast_ablation.csv, forecast_predictions.csv). Repo untouched.

## Phase 3: Build the deck
Five slides on one theme, matching the outline.

Revised after feedback on 5 October: academic style, written to the no-ai-slop rules, six slides.

| # | Slide | Title | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evaluation setup | Every model is tested on a study it has not seen (inputs, model, output, leave-one-study-out) | 1:00 |
| 2 | ML vs the curve | No ML model has lower error than the duration curve | 1:00 |
| 3 | LLM system view | LLM forecast: what is sent and what comes back (real cached request and answer) | 1:30 |
| 4 | LLM vs all models | The LLM forecast has the lowest error of all models | 0:45 |
| 5 | Validity checks | Validity checks on the LLM forecast (bullets) | 1:15 |
| 6 | Wrap-up | Conclusions, limitations and further work | 0:30 |

**Done when:**
- Six slides, in this order, each with a visual (diagram, chart or table), not just bullets.
- Body text is 14 pt or larger, readable from the back of a room.
- Speaker notes on every slide, written to be said aloud in about the slide's time.
- The 15% target isn't on any slide (it's in the notes as a prepared answer).
- The caveat that the LLM was added after the ML result is said in the notes for slide 4.

**Status:** done. `technical-half.pptx`, generated by `build-technical-half.js`.

## Phase 4: Quality checks
**Done when:**

**Status:** done. Validator passes; all 5 slides rendered and inspected; no occurrence of the product or company name anywhere in the file; every number checked against `results/`.
